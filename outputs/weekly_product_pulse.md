# Weekly Product Pulse

28 July–19 October 2026. 12,800 English Google Play reviews for Groww in India. Themes are keyword matches, not semantic clusters. Matches are not customer shares.

## Top three themes

1. **Customer support:** 260 of 12,800 reviews (2.0%), 217 of them 1–2 star. Among 3,610 longer reviews, 257 match (7.1%).
2. **Brokerage and charges:** 275 of 12,800 (2.1%); 252 of 3,610 longer reviews (7.0%). This is not a fee audit.
3. **Ease of use:** 441 of 12,800 (3.4%), 407 of them 4–5 star. Of the longer reviews, 333 match (9.2%).

## Three reviews

- `30fc6cf8-2776-4b89-b27e-78bb96f38d0b`, 21 September 2026, 1 star: “Worst customer care service, no reply for last few of my complaints till now”
- `80902ca3-7757-4566-a348-c18b33fd04d6`, 19 October 2026, 1 star: “Brokerage charges very high after 2025... why?”
- `44cf2908-a553-4a3b-8909-604f853d4b63`, 28 August 2026, 4 star: “This app has a clean user interface and is very easy to use.”

## Key observation

Short generic praise inflates the 77.1% five-star share. Support and charges recur as complaints in longer reviews, and ease of use recurs as praise.

## Three product ideas

1. Show complaint reply status. The 21 September review says there was no reply.
2. Link charge questions to the official explanation. The 19 October review asks why brokerage is high. That does not prove the fee is wrong.
3. Keep the simple interface. The 28 August review asks that a cancelled or rejected SIP disappear after 30 days, and that sold orders leave the active holdings view.
