# Fee Explainer

Last checked: 21 October 2026

This note records charges published by Groww. It is not investment advice. A customer review that brokerage is high does not, by itself, show that a fee was charged in error.

- Equity brokerage on Groww’s pricing page is the lower of ₹20 or 0.1% per executed order, with a minimum of ₹5. The delivery-charges help page states the same lower-of-₹20-or-0.1% rule and a ₹5 minimum. [Pricing](https://groww.in/pricing/) [Delivery charges](https://groww.in/help/stocks,-f&o,-ipo-&-mtf/sx-pricing/what-is-the-charge-for-buy-order-delivery)
- That brokerage line is not the full cost. The pricing page lists separate regulatory and statutory charges, including securities transaction tax, stamp duty, exchange transaction charges, the SEBI turnover charge, and the Investor Protection Fund Trust charge.
- The pricing page states 18% GST on brokerage, DP charges, exchange transaction charges, the Investor Protection Fund Trust charge, and the SEBI turnover charge, among other listed items. The delivery-charges page says this GST is not applied to securities transaction tax or stamp duty.
- For a delivery sell, both pages list a depository participant charge: depository ₹3.50 for male investors or ₹3.25 for female investors, plus Groww ₹16.50. Groww’s portion is ₹0 when the debit value is under ₹100. GST is added.
- The pricing page states a different equity brokerage when the UPI Mandate balance is used: 1% of the order value, with no maximum cap.
- Groww’s help page on money on hold says the order amount and estimated charges are held, deducted if the order is executed, and released if it is not executed. The pricing page describes equity brokerage per executed order. [Money on hold](https://groww.in/help/stocks,-f&o,-ipo-&-mtf/order/why-is-money-on-hold-for-stocks)

## Unresolved gaps

- The help article “How much is the brokerage?” describes the sub-₹5 minimum as the lower of ₹5 or 2.5% of trade value. The pricing page and the delivery-charges page say the minimum is ₹5. Those wordings were not reconciled, so the 2.5% minimum is not used above. [How much is the brokerage?](https://groww.in/help/stocks,-f&o,-ipo-&-mtf/searchable/how-much-is-the-brokerage)
- That same article states F&O brokerage of ₹20 per order. The pricing-page text checked here did not restate that F&O figure, so it is not included in the bullets.
- No page checked here itemises a brokerage amount for a cancelled order beyond “per executed order” and the release of a hold when an order is not executed.
- The pricing page heading “Zero Hidden Charges” is not treated as a finding. The same page lists charges other than brokerage.
